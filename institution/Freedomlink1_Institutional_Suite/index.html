    🏛️ Freedomlink1 Sovereign Purpose Trust Charter

    PREAMBLE

    Let it be known that on this day, the Founder establishes the Freedomlink1 Sovereign Purpose Trust as a civic‑grade, lineage‑anchored institution dedicated to the protection, advancement, and ceremonial continuity of the Freedomlink1 governance system.

    This Trust exists not for private gain, but for the enduring public purpose of maintaining the Freedomlink1 lineage, its hardware‑rooted architecture, its sovereign codices, its epoch advancement ceremonies, and its institutional memory.

    I. DEFINITIONS

    Trust: Freedomlink1 Sovereign Purpose Trust
    Founder: Joshua W. Blaz
    Trustee: Fiduciary appointed to administer the Trust
    Enforcer: Guardian ensuring the Trust’s purpose is upheld
    Purpose: Preservation and advancement of Freedomlink1
    Trust Property: All assets listed in Schedule A
    Agency: Operational arm owned by the Trust
    Lineage Archive: Permanent repository of all Freedomlink1 inscriptions

    II. ESTABLISHMENT OF THE TRUST

    The Trust is established as an Irrevocable Purpose Trust under Wyoming law.
    The Trust is irrevocable.
    The Trust shall endure in perpetuity.

    III. PURPOSE OF THE TRUST

    The Trust exists to:

    • Preserve the Freedomlink1 lineage
    • Maintain manuscripts, glyphs, seals, ribbons, monograms
    • Govern the Freedomlink1 Creator Token
    • Hold and manage crypto‑treasury assets
    • Oversee Rootstone‑I hardware root of trust
    • Maintain the governance codex
    • Ensure continuity across cycles and generations
    • Support the Freedomlink1 Agency

    IV. TRUSTEE

    Appointment

    The Founder appoints Joshua W. Blaz as Trustee.

    Powers

    The Trustee may:

    • Hold and manage Trust Property
    • Operate crypto wallets
    • Execute agreements
    • Oversee the Agency
    • Maintain financial accounts

    Limitations

    The Trustee may not:

    • Alter ceremonial manuscripts
    • Modify lineage inscriptions
    • Issue tokens without authorization
    • Dissolve the Trust

    V. ENFORCER

    Appointment

    The Founder appoints the Sovereign Governance Enforcer.

    Powers

    The Enforcer may:

    • Remove or replace the Trustee
    • Approve amendments
    • Validate ceremonial seals
    • Audit treasury operations

    VI. BENEFICIARIES

    The Trust has no private beneficiaries.
    Its beneficiaries are the public purpose of Freedomlink1.

    VII. TRUST PROPERTY

    Includes:

    Intellectual Property

    Manuscripts
    Codex
    Glyphs, seals, ribbons
    Hardware designs
    Software repositories

    Digital Assets

    BTC, ETH, SOL, USDC wallets
    Multi‑sig governance wallets
    Creator Token
    Smart contracts

    Physical Assets

    Rootstone‑I prototypes
    Ceremonial artifacts

    Agency Ownership

    The Trust owns 100% of the Freedomlink1 Agency.

    VIII. AMENDMENTS

    Require:

    • Trustee approval
    • Enforcer approval
    • Ceremonial seal
    • Lineage inscription

    Immutable:

    • Trust purpose
    • Public‑benefit nature
    • Prohibition on private gain

    IX. DISSOLUTION

    Only possible if:

    • Trustee + Enforcer agree
    • Purpose becomes impossible
    • Assets transfer to successor institution

    X. CRYPTO‑TREASURY CLAUSES

    Trust‑owned wallets
    Multi‑sig governance
    Transparency dashboards
    Token governance
    Hardware‑root integration

    XI. CEREMONIAL CLAUSES

    Founder’s monogram
    Cycle seals
    Ribbon text

    XII. GOVERNING LAW

    State of Wyoming.
