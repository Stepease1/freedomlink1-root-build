# Module Activation Ceremony

## Freedomlink1 - Genesis Epoch

This ceremony records the controlled activation of a module after lineage and governance review.

---

## I. Presentation

- Ceremony ID: MOD-ACT-002
- Module registry: `lineage/module_registry.json`
- Activation epoch: Genesis

## II. Verification

Custodians verify the module identity, source hash, domain alignment, and authorization under the permissions matrix.

## III. Activation Declaration

The Steward declares the module active only after the registry, signature, and ceremony records are complete.

## IV. Closing Record

Record the decision in `lineage/ceremonies.md` and regenerate the applicable integrity artifacts.
