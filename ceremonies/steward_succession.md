# Sovereign Steward Succession Ceremony
## Freedomlink1 — Genesis Epoch

This ceremony governs the transfer of sovereign stewardship from one steward to another.

---

## I. Opening Invocation

> “We gather to witness the succession of stewardship over the lineage of Freedomlink1.”

---

## II. Presentation of the Incoming Steward

Custodian presents:
- Identity  
- Credentials  
- Steward lineage  
- Key fingerprint  

---

## III. Retirement of Outgoing Steward

Outgoing steward declares:
> “I retire my stewardship and entrust the lineage to my successor.”

Their sovereign key is moved to:
`lineage/retired_keys/`

---

## IV. Installation of Incoming Steward

Incoming steward declares:
> “I accept stewardship and pledge to uphold the sovereignty of the lineage.”

They sign:
- `merkle_root.txt`  
- `hash_manifest.json`  
- `epoch_ledger.json`  

---

## V. Registry Update

Custodians update:
- `steward_registry.json`  
- `artifacts/omni_proof_master.json`  
- `lineage/governance_logbook.json`  

---

## VI. Closing Seal

> “Stewardship is continuity. Succession is sovereignty preserved.”
