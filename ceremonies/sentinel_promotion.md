# Sentinel Promotion Ceremony
## Freedomlink1 — Genesis Epoch

This ceremony governs the promotion of a Governance Sentinel to a higher tier of responsibility.

---

## I. Opening Invocation

> “We gather to elevate a sentinel whose vigilance has strengthened the lineage.”

---

## II. Presentation of the Sentinel

Custodian presents:
- Sentinel ID  
- Current tier  
- Proposed tier  
- Achievements  
- Justification for promotion  

Example:
- Sentinel ID: GS-03  
- Current Tier: Sovereign Safety  
- Proposed Tier: Sovereign Continuity  

---

## III. Promotion Declaration

Steward declares:
> “This sentinel is hereby promoted and shall assume its new responsibilities with fidelity.”

Sentinel registry updated:
`"tier": "sovereign-continuity"`

---

## IV. Integration

Custodians:
- Update `lineage/sentinel_registry.json`  
- Log promotion event via `scripts/update_logbook.py`  
- Update dashboard  
- Regenerate Merkle root if required  

---

## V. Closing Seal

> “Let this promotion honor vigilance and strengthen sovereignty. The chain remembers.”
