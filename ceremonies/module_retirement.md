# Module Retirement Ceremony
## Freedomlink1 — Genesis Epoch

This ceremony governs the retirement of modules that have been superseded, deprecated, or replaced.

---

## I. Opening Invocation

> “We gather to retire a module whose service to the lineage is complete.”

---

## II. Presentation of the Module

Custodian reads:
- Module ID  
- Origin POC  
- Domain alignment  
- Activation epoch  
- Reason for retirement  

---

## III. Retirement Declaration

Steward declares:
> “This module is hereby retired and shall no longer participate in governance or lineage operations.”

Module status updated to:
`"retired"`

---

## IV. Artifact Archival

Custodians:
- Move module artifacts to `archive/modules/`  
- Update `module_registry.json`  
- Regenerate Merkle root  
- Update proof master  

---

## V. Closing Seal

> “Let this retirement honor continuity and preserve the lineage. The chain remembers.”
