# Sentinel Activation Ceremony
## Freedomlink1 — Genesis Epoch

This ceremony marks the activation of a new Governance Sentinel within the Root Build.

---

## I. Opening Invocation

> “We gather to activate a sentinel whose purpose is vigilance, continuity, and protection of the lineage.”

---

## II. Presentation of the Sentinel

Custodian presents:
- Sentinel ID  
- Purpose  
- Trigger conditions  
- Monitoring scope  
- Notification channels  

Example:
- Sentinel ID: GS-03  
- Purpose: Root Build Integrity Monitoring  
- Scope: Lineage baseline, corruption detection, drift detection  

---

## III. Activation Declaration

Steward declares:
> “This sentinel is hereby activated and shall watch over the lineage with autonomy and fidelity.”

Sentinel status updated to:
`"active"`

---

## IV. Integration

Custodians:
- Add sentinel to Governance Sentinel Registry  
- Publish activation event to governance_logbook.json  
- Update integrity dashboard  

---

## V. Closing Seal

> “Let this sentinel guard continuity and uphold sovereignty. The chain remembers.”
