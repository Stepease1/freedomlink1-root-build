# 🏛️ Freedomlink1 Agency Charter
Operational Arm of the Trust

## PREAMBLE

The Agency executes the mission defined by the Trust and Codex.

## I. NAME AND DOMICILE

Name: Freedomlink1 Agency  
Parent: Freedomlink1 Sovereign Purpose Trust  
Grantor EIN: 92‑1026132  
Domicile: Oklahoma

## II. MISSION

The Agency shall:

- Execute governance operations
- Maintain hardware-rooted architectures
- Operate software stack and build systems
- Publish documentation and lineage artifacts
- Manage treasury operations
- Support epoch advancement
- Provide public‑benefit services

## III. STRUCTURE

### Ownership

Wholly owned by the Trust.

### Authority

Acts under:

- Trustee
- Enforcer
- Codex
- Trust Charter

### Independence

May operate infrastructure, repositories, hardware, documentation.  
May not alter lineage or issue tokens without approval.

## IV. FUNCTIONS

Governance operations  
Technical operations  
Treasury operations  
Public interface

## V. APPOINTMENTS

Trustee appoints Agency Director.  
Officers may be appointed for treasury, hardware, software, documentation, governance.

## VI. AMENDMENTS

Require Trustee + Enforcer + Codex compliance.

## VII. DISSOLUTION

Only if Trust dissolves or successor institution is created.
