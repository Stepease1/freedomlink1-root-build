## II. MULTI‑SIG GOVERNANCE

### Configuration

Keys held by:

- Trustee
- Enforcer
- Agency Director
- Hardware Officer

Incoming require none.  
Emergency freeze: Trustee + Enforcer.

### Key Rotation

Annually or upon officer change.
