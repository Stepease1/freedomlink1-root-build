# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (DPA)
**Freedomlink1 — Sovereign Digital Institution**  
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026
**Epoch:** 2 (Root Build v1.2)
**Prepared by:** Joshua W. Blaz

## 1. Purpose
This Data Processing Addendum (“Addendum” or “DPA”) supplements any agreement between **Freedomlink1** (“Institution”) and the **Processor** regarding the handling of personal, sensitive, or regulated data.

The DPA ensures that all data processing activities:

- comply with applicable privacy and security laws
- respect sovereign governance and lineage protocols
- preserve hardware trust boundaries
- maintain institutional integrity and confidentiality

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 “Institution”
Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, sovereign layers, hardware trust boundaries, documentation, and lineage artifacts.

### 2.2 “Processor”
The individual or entity processing data on behalf of the Institution.

### 2.3 “Controller”
The Institution, which determines the purposes and means of data processing.

### 2.4 “Data Subject”
Any individual whose personal data is processed under this Addendum.

### 2.5 “Personal Data”
Any information relating to an identified or identifiable Data Subject.

### 2.6 “Processing”
Any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, retrieval, transmission, deletion, or destruction.

### 2.7 “Sovereign Layer”
The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Controller Responsibilities
The Institution:

- determines the purpose and means of processing
- ensures lawful basis for processing
- provides instructions to the Processor
- maintains sovereign lineage records where applicable

### 3.2 Processor Responsibilities
The Processor shall:

- process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Institution
- ensure confidentiality and security
- comply with applicable privacy laws
- maintain accurate records of processing activities
- notify the Institution of any data incidents

## 4. Processing Instructions
Processor shall process Personal Data solely for:

- operational support
- technical services
- analytics or reporting
- governance module execution
- hardware trust operations
- documentation or institutional workflows

Processor shall not:

- use Personal Data for its own purposes
- sell or transfer Personal Data
- modify sovereign lineage records
- bypass hardware trust boundaries

## 5. Security Measures
Processor shall implement:

- encryption in transit and at rest
- access controls and authentication
- secure storage and deletion protocols
- hardware‑rooted trust mechanisms where applicable
- audit logging and monitoring
- incident detection and response procedures

Security measures must align with the Institution’s sovereign governance and hardware trust architecture.

## 6. Sub‑Processors
Processor shall not engage sub‑processors without:

- prior written approval from the Institution
- ensuring sub‑processors comply with this DPA
- maintaining a list of authorized sub‑processors

## 7. Data Subject Rights
Processor shall assist the Institution in responding to:

- access requests
- correction requests
- deletion requests
- restriction or objection requests
- data portability requests

Processor shall not respond directly to Data Subjects unless authorized.

## 8. Data Breach Notification
Processor shall notify the Institution **without undue delay** upon becoming aware of:

- unauthorized access
- accidental loss
- destruction
- alteration
- disclosure of Personal Data

Notification shall include:

- nature of the breach
- categories and number of Data Subjects affected
- likely consequences
- mitigation measures taken

## 9. Data Transfers
Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside approved jurisdictions without:

- lawful transfer mechanisms
- encryption
- sovereign governance approval
- lineage recording where applicable

## 10. Return or Deletion of Data
Upon termination of services, Processor shall:

- return all Personal Data to the Institution, or
- securely delete all Personal Data

Deletion must comply with sovereign governance and hardware trust requirements.

## 11. Sovereign Compliance
Processor acknowledges and agrees that:

- certain processing activities may be recorded in sovereign lineage
- hardware trust boundaries may define operational authority
- sovereign signatures may be required for compliance events
- institutional proclamations may define future governance changes

## 12. Audit Rights
Institution may audit Processor’s compliance with this DPA, including:

- security controls
- data handling procedures
- sub‑processor compliance
- lineage‑related operations

Audits may be conducted by the Institution or an authorized third party.

## 13. Governing Law
This DPA is governed by the laws of the **State of Oklahoma**, without regard to conflict‑of‑law principles.

## 14. Term and Termination
This DPA remains in effect as long as Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of the Institution.

Upon termination:

- Processor shall cease processing
- return or delete Personal Data
- maintain confidentiality obligations
