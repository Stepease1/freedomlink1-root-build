## INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

**Freedomlink1 - Sovereign Digital Institution**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** March 8, 2026  
**Epoch:** 2 (Root Build v1.2)  
**Prepared by:** Joshua W. Blaz

## 1. Purpose

This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement ("Agreement") ensures that all intellectual property ("IP") created by the **Contributor** ("Assignor") for **Freedomlink1** ("Institution") is fully and permanently assigned to the Institution.

This Agreement protects:

- sovereign lineage continuity
- hardware trust boundaries
- governance integrity
- institutional ownership
- cryptographic legitimacy
- documentation consistency

It ensures that all contributions become part of the Institution's permanent sovereign corpus.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 "Institution"

Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, lineage artifacts, hardware trust layer, documentation, and sovereign state.

### 2.2 "Assignor"

Any individual or entity contributing IP to the Institution.

### 2.3 "Assigned IP"

All intellectual property created, authored, designed, developed, or produced by the Assignor for the Institution, including:

- software
- hardware schematics
- Rootstone-I related designs
- governance modules
- lineage artifacts
- documentation
- specifications
- CI logs, test results, and simulation outputs
- cryptographic materials, excluding private keys
- design assets
- research
- prototypes

### 2.4 "Pre-Existing IP"

IP owned by the Assignor prior to this Agreement.

### 2.5 "Sovereign Layer"

The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.

## 3. Assignment of Intellectual Property

### 3.1 Full Assignment

Assignor hereby assigns to the Institution all rights, title, and interest in the Assigned IP, including:

- copyrights
- trade secrets
- design rights
- technical schematics
- documentation
- derivative works
- improvements
- modifications

This assignment is **perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, and exclusive**.

### 3.2 Deliverables Included

Assigned IP includes all Deliverables produced under:

- MSA
- PSA
- SOWs
- advisory engagements
- contributor agreements
- informal collaboration
- sovereign resolutions

### 3.3 Automatic Assignment

Any IP created by the Assignor **during** or **related to** work for the Institution is automatically assigned upon creation.

## 4. Pre-Existing IP

### 4.1 Ownership Retained

Assignor retains ownership of Pre-Existing IP.

### 4.2 License to Institution

Assignor grants the Institution a:

- perpetual
- irrevocable
- royalty-free
- worldwide

license to use Pre-Existing IP **as necessary** for the Assigned IP.

### 4.3 No Restrictions

Assignor may not impose licensing restrictions that limit the Institution's use of Assigned IP.

## 5. Moral Rights

### 5.1 Waiver

Assignor waives all moral rights to the Assigned IP, including:

- attribution
- integrity
- disclosure
- withdrawal

to the extent permitted by law.

### 5.2 Cooperation

Assignor agrees to execute additional documents to perfect the Institution's ownership.

## 6. Confidentiality

Assignor shall comply with the Institution's NDA, including:

- confidentiality obligations
- non-use restrictions
- protection of sovereign materials
- return or destruction requirements

Confidentiality obligations survive termination.

## 7. Representations and Warranties

Assignor represents and warrants that:

- Assigned IP is original
- Assigned IP does not infringe third-party rights
- Assigned IP is free of malicious code
- Assignor has full authority to assign the IP
- Assigned IP was not created using unauthorized materials

## 8. Indemnification

Assignor shall indemnify the Institution against:

- IP infringement claims
- breach of confidentiality
- breach of assignment obligations
- unauthorized use of third-party materials

## 9. Sovereign Compliance

### 9.1 Governance Requirements

Assignor must comply with:

- lineage anchoring rules
- hardware trust boundaries
- governance primitives
- sovereign signature protocols

### 9.2 Prohibited Actions

Assignor may not:

- alter lineage artifacts
- bypass Rootstone-I trust boundaries
- modify governance modules without authorization
- access sovereign keys
- introduce cryptographic anomalies

## 10. Term and Termination

### 10.1 Term

This Agreement remains in effect indefinitely for Assigned IP.

### 10.2 Termination

Termination does not affect:

- IP already assigned
- confidentiality obligations
- sovereign compliance obligations

## 11. Dispute Resolution

Disputes shall be resolved through:

1. discussion
2. mediation
3. sovereign signature block
4. inclusion in the next epoch ledger

If unresolved, the Founder may issue a binding proclamation.

## 12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Oklahoma**, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

## 13. Amendment

This Agreement may be amended only through:

- written approval
- sovereign signature
- publication in the next epoch ledger
