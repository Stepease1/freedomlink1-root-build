## 1. Purpose
This Simple Agreement for Future Equity (“SAFE”) provides the Investor with the right to receive equity in **Freedomlink1** (“Institution”) upon the occurrence of a future equity financing or liquidity event.

This SAFE is designed for sovereign digital institutions and incorporates lineage, governance, and hardware‑rooted trust principles.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 “Institution”
Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, sovereign layers, hardware trust boundaries, documentation, and lineage artifacts.

### 2.2 “Investor”
The individual or entity providing funds under this SAFE.

### 2.3 “Purchase Amount”
The amount invested by the Investor under this SAFE.

### 2.4 “Equity Financing”
A bona fide transaction or series of transactions with the principal purpose of raising capital, in which the Institution issues preferred or common equity.

### 2.5 “Liquidity Event”
A change‑of‑control transaction, merger, acquisition, or other event resulting in the transfer of ownership or control of the Institution.

### 2.6 “Valuation Cap”
The maximum institutional valuation used to determine the Investor’s conversion price.

### 2.7 “Discount Rate”
The percentage discount applied to the Investor’s conversion price relative to the price paid by new investors in an Equity Financing.

### 2.8 “Sovereign Layer”
The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.

## 4. Conversion to Equity

### 4.1 Equity Financing Conversion
Upon an Equity Financing, the Purchase Amount automatically converts into equity at the **lower** of:

- the price per share implied by the **Valuation Cap**, or
- the price per share discounted by the **Discount Rate**.

### 4.2 Liquidity Event Conversion
If a Liquidity Event occurs before an Equity Financing, the Investor will receive:

- cash equal to the Purchase Amount, **or**
- equity equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the Valuation Cap price per share,
whichever is greater.

### 4.3 No Interest
The SAFE does not accrue interest.
