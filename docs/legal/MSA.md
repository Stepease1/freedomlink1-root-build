## MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)

**Freedomlink1 - Sovereign Digital Institution**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** March 8, 2026  
**Epoch:** 2 (Root Build v1.2)  
**Prepared by:** Joshua W. Blaz

## 1. Purpose

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") governs the relationship between **Freedomlink1** ("Institution") and the **Service Provider** ("Provider") for the delivery of technical, operational, advisory, engineering, design, or governance-related services.

The Agreement ensures that all work performed for the Institution respects:

- sovereign lineage
- hardware trust boundaries
- governance integrity
- confidentiality
- IP ownership
- institutional continuity

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 "Institution"

Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, lineage artifacts, hardware trust layer, documentation, and sovereign state.

### 2.2 "Provider"

Any individual or entity performing services under this Agreement.

### 2.3 "Services"

All work performed by the Provider, including:

- engineering
- software development
- hardware design
- documentation
- governance module creation
- testing, simulation, and CI integration
- advisory or consulting work
- operational support

### 2.4 "Deliverables"

Any output produced by the Provider, including:

- code
- hardware schematics
- documentation
- lineage artifacts
- test results
- CI logs
- design materials
- specifications

### 2.5 "Confidential Information"

All non-public information belonging to the Institution, as defined in the NDA.

### 2.6 "Sovereign Layer"

The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.

## 3. Scope of Services

### 3.1 Engagement

The Institution engages the Provider to perform Services as described in:

- Statements of Work ("SOWs")
- written instructions
- sovereign resolutions
- project briefs

### 3.2 Changes to Scope

Scope changes require:

- written approval
- sovereign signature, if material
- updated SOW

### 3.3 Performance Standard

Provider shall perform Services:

- professionally
- diligently
- in accordance with industry standards
- in alignment with sovereign governance requirements

## 4. Deliverables

### 4.1 Requirements

Deliverables must:

- meet specifications
- be complete and functional
- integrate with the Institution's systems
- comply with hardware trust boundaries
- comply with lineage anchoring requirements

### 4.2 Acceptance

Deliverables are accepted when:

- reviewed by the Institution
- confirmed to meet requirements
- integrated into the sovereign lineage, if applicable

### 4.3 Rejection

The Institution may reject Deliverables that:

- fail requirements
- introduce security risks
- violate sovereign trust boundaries
- conflict with governance primitives

Provider must correct deficiencies at no additional cost.

## 5. Compensation

### 5.1 Payment Terms

Compensation shall be defined in each SOW and may include:

- fixed fees
- hourly rates
- milestone payments
- equity instruments: SAFE, SPA, or similar instruments

### 5.2 Invoices

Provider shall submit invoices according to the SOW.

### 5.3 Non-Payment

The Institution may withhold payment if:

- Deliverables are incomplete
- Services violate this Agreement
- confidentiality is breached

## 6. Intellectual Property

### 6.1 Ownership

All Deliverables and related IP are owned exclusively by the Institution.

### 6.2 Assignment

Provider hereby assigns all rights, title, and interest in Deliverables to the Institution.

### 6.3 Pre-Existing Materials

Provider retains ownership of pre-existing materials but grants the Institution a perpetual, irrevocable license to use them as part of Deliverables.

### 6.4 No Restrictions

Provider may not impose licensing restrictions on Deliverables.

## 7. Confidentiality

Provider shall comply with the Institution's NDA, including:

- confidentiality obligations
- non-use restrictions
- protection of sovereign materials
- return or destruction requirements

Confidentiality obligations survive termination.

## 8. Security and Sovereign Compliance

### 8.1 Security Requirements

Provider must:

- protect cryptographic materials
- safeguard hardware schematics
- secure lineage artifacts
- follow sovereign governance protocols

### 8.2 Prohibited Actions

Provider may not:

- bypass hardware trust boundaries
- alter lineage artifacts without authorization
- access sovereign keys
- modify governance primitives

### 8.3 Incident Reporting

Provider must immediately report:

- security breaches
- unauthorized access
- data loss
- cryptographic anomalies

## 9. Warranties

Provider warrants that:

- Deliverables are original
- Services do not infringe third-party rights
- Deliverables are free of malicious code
- work will be performed professionally

## 10. Indemnification

Provider shall indemnify the Institution against:

- IP infringement claims
- negligence
- breach of confidentiality
- breach of sovereign compliance

## 11. Term and Termination

### 11.1 Term

This Agreement remains in effect until terminated.

### 11.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate with 30 days' notice.

### 11.3 Termination for Cause

The Institution may terminate immediately if Provider:

- breaches confidentiality
- violates sovereign trust boundaries
- fails to deliver
- engages in misconduct

### 11.4 Effect of Termination

Provider must:

- cease all work
- return Confidential Information
- deliver completed work
- certify destruction of sensitive materials

## 12. Dispute Resolution

Disputes shall be resolved through:

1. discussion
2. mediation
3. sovereign signature block
4. inclusion in the next epoch ledger

If unresolved, the Founder may issue a binding proclamation.

## 13. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Oklahoma**, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

## 14. Amendment

This Agreement may be amended only through:

- written approval
- sovereign signature
- publication in the next epoch ledger
