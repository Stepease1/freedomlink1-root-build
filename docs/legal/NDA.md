## NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

**Freedomlink1 - Sovereign Digital Institution**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** March 8, 2026  
**Epoch:** 2 (Root Build v1.2)  
**Prepared by:** Joshua W. Blaz

## 1. Purpose

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") governs the protection of confidential, sovereign, technical, institutional, and lineage-anchored information belonging to **Freedomlink1**, including hardware schematics, governance modules, cryptographic materials, documentation, and unreleased artifacts.

The Receiving Party agrees to maintain strict confidentiality and use such information solely for authorized evaluation or collaboration with the Institution.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 "Institution"

Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, lineage artifacts, hardware trust layer, documentation, and sovereign state.

### 2.2 "Disclosing Party"

Freedomlink1, represented by its Founder or authorized steward.

### 2.3 "Receiving Party"

Any individual or entity receiving Confidential Information under this Agreement.

### 2.4 "Confidential Information"

Any non-public information disclosed by the Institution, including:

- hardware schematics, including Rootstone-I
- fuse maps, enclave configurations, and electrical characteristics
- governance logic, ceremony engines, and seal pathways
- lineage artifacts: ledger, hash manifest, Merkle roots, and signature blocks
- institutional documents: legal, operational, and architectural
- cryptographic keys, secrets, and attestation materials
- CI logs, simulation outputs, and internal testing artifacts
- unreleased software, hardware, or documentation
- strategic plans, roadmaps, and institutional decisions

Confidential Information includes all forms: written, digital, oral, schematic, cryptographic, or ceremonial.

### 2.5 "Sovereign Layer"

The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

### 3.1 Confidentiality

The Receiving Party shall:

- maintain strict confidentiality
- use Confidential Information only for authorized evaluation
- restrict access to individuals with a legitimate need
- protect all materials with reasonable and sovereign-grade security

### 3.2 Non-Use

The Receiving Party shall not:

- reverse engineer
- decompile
- analyze cryptographic materials
- derive hardware schematics
- replicate governance logic
- create derivative works

unless explicitly authorized in writing.

### 3.3 Non-Disclosure

The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without:

- written approval
- a sovereign signature
- inclusion in the next epoch ledger, if applicable

### 3.4 Protection of Sovereign Materials

The Receiving Party must treat lineage artifacts, hardware codices, and cryptographic materials with the highest level of protection.

## 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

- is publicly available without breach
- is independently developed without reference to Confidential Information
- is lawfully obtained from a third party without restriction
- is required to be disclosed by law, with immediate notice to the Institution

## 5. Ownership

All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of the Institution. No license or rights are granted except as explicitly stated.

## 6. Return or Destruction

Upon request, the Receiving Party shall:

- return all Confidential Information
- destroy all copies
- certify destruction
- remove all derived materials from devices, storage, and backups

This obligation survives termination.

## 7. Term

This Agreement remains in effect for **five (5) years** from the Effective Date, and confidentiality obligations survive indefinitely for:

- cryptographic materials
- hardware schematics
- lineage artifacts
- unreleased governance modules
- institutional strategy

## 8. Remedies

The Institution is entitled to:

- injunctive relief
- equitable remedies
- damages
- sovereign arbitration (Section 10)

Monetary damages alone may be insufficient.

## 9. No Warranty

Confidential Information is provided "as-is," without warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for purpose.

## 10. Sovereign Arbitration

Disputes shall be resolved through:

1. discussion
2. mediation
3. sovereign signature block
4. inclusion in the next epoch ledger

If unresolved, the Founder may issue a proclamation binding on all parties.

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Oklahoma**, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

## 12. Amendment

This Agreement may be amended only through:

- written approval
- sovereign signature
- publication in the next epoch ledger

## 13. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding Confidential Information.
