## 1. Purpose
This Share Purchase Agreement (“Agreement”) governs the purchase and sale of equity shares in **Freedomlink1** (“Institution”) by the **Purchaser**.

The Agreement ensures that all equity transactions:

- respect sovereign lineage
- preserve governance integrity
- align with hardware trust boundaries
- maintain institutional continuity
- properly record ownership in sovereign layers

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 “Institution”
Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, sovereign layers, hardware trust boundaries, documentation, and lineage artifacts.

### 2.2 “Purchaser”
The individual or entity acquiring shares under this Agreement.

### 2.3 “Shares”
The equity units of the Institution being purchased.

### 2.4 “Purchase Price”
The total amount paid by the Purchaser for the Shares.

### 2.5 “Closing Date”
The date on which the purchase and sale of Shares is completed.

### 2.6 “Sovereign Layer”
The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.
