# 🏛️ Freedomlink1 Agency Charter
Operational Arm of the Freedomlink1 Sovereign Purpose Trust
EIN 92‑1026132 (Nonprofit Grantor)

## PREAMBLE

The Freedomlink1 Agency is hereby established as the operational arm of the Freedomlink1 Sovereign Purpose Trust, empowered to execute the mission, maintain the systems, and uphold the lineage of the Freedomlink1 institution.

Where the Trust governs, the Agency acts.  
Where the Codex defines, the Agency implements.  
Where the lineage advances, the Agency witnesses.

## I. NAME AND DOMICILE

**Name:** Freedomlink1 Agency  
**Parent Entity:** Freedomlink1 Sovereign Purpose Trust  
**Grantor EIN:** 92‑1026132  
**Domicile:** Oklahoma, United States  
**Governing Law:** Wyoming Purpose Trust Statutes + Oklahoma Nonprofit Law

## II. MISSION

The mission of the Freedomlink1 Agency is to:

- Execute operational tasks defined by the Trust and Codex
- Maintain hardware-rooted architectures (Rootstone‑I and successors)
- Operate the Freedomlink1 software stack, repositories, and build systems
- Publish governance documentation, codices, ceremonies, and lineage artifacts
- Manage treasury operations under multi‑sig governance
- Support epoch advancement, cycle seals, and institutional continuity
- Provide public‑benefit services aligned with nonprofit requirements

The Agency shall never operate for private gain.

## III. STRUCTURE

### 3.1 Ownership

The Agency is wholly owned by the Freedomlink1 Sovereign Purpose Trust.

### 3.2 Operational Authority

The Agency acts under the direction of:

- The Trustee
- The Enforcer
- The Sovereign Codex
- The Trust Charter

### 3.3 Independence

The Agency may:

- enter contracts
- operate infrastructure
- manage repositories
- maintain hardware
- publish documentation

But may not:

- alter lineage inscriptions
- modify immutable codex sections
- issue tokens without treasury approval

## IV. FUNCTIONS

### 4.1 Governance Operations

- Maintain governance logic
- Execute amendments
- Publish cycle seals
- Manage lineage archives

### 4.2 Technical Operations

- Maintain GitHub repositories
- Manage build systems
- Operate Rootstone‑I firmware pipelines
- Maintain sovereign hardware designs

### 4.3 Treasury Operations

- Execute multi‑sig transactions
- Maintain transparency dashboards
- Publish treasury reports
- Implement funding logic

### 4.4 Public Interface

- Publish documentation
- Provide public updates
- Maintain institutional website
- Respond to governance inquiries

## V. APPOINTMENTS

### 5.1 Agency Director

The Trustee appoints the Agency Director.

### 5.2 Officers

The Agency may appoint officers for:

- Treasury
- Hardware
- Software
- Documentation
- Governance

## VI. AMENDMENTS

Amendments require:

- Trustee approval
- Enforcer approval
- Codex compliance
- Lineage inscription

## VII. DISSOLUTION

The Agency may only be dissolved if:

- The Trust dissolves
- A successor institution is established
- All lineage assets are transferred
