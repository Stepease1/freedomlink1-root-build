# ✦ Governance Review Cycle  
Freedomlink1 Sovereign Institution • Governance Process Layer

The Governance Review Cycle defines how proposals move through the Freedomlink1 institution.  
It ensures that all governance actions are sovereign, consistent, lineage‑anchored, and compliant with the Institutional Suite, Codex, PPTF, and Unified Layer.

This page provides a public, structured explanation of the full governance process.

---

## ✦ Overview of the Cycle

The Governance Review Cycle consists of six sovereign phases:

1. **Submission**  
2. **Validation**  
3. **Codex Compliance Review**  
4. **Lineage Continuity Review**  
5. **Treasury & PPTF Review**  
6. **Unified Layer Integration Review**  
7. **Sovereign Approval & Inscription**  

Each phase ensures institutional stability and continuity.

---

## 🜁 Phase I — Submission

Proposals enter the institution through the **Proposal Submission Portal**.

Submitters must provide:

- title  
- summary  
- rationale  
- codex compliance  
- lineage impact  
- treasury impact  
- implementation plan  
- amendment requirements  

Once submitted, proposals are added to the governance index.

---

## 🜂 Phase II — Validation

The Proposal Validation Workflow performs:

- metadata checks  
- required section checks  
- structural integrity checks  
- sovereign formatting checks  

Invalid proposals are rejected with required corrections.

Valid proposals advance to Codex Review.

---

## 🜃 Phase III — Codex Compliance Review

Codex Review ensures:

- clauses are correctly referenced  
- amendments follow sovereign rules  
- manuscript continuity is preserved  
- codex bindings remain consistent  

Codex‑compliant proposals advance to Lineage Review.

---

## 🜄 Phase IV — Lineage Continuity Review

Lineage Review ensures:

- epoch references are valid  
- anchors are correctly mapped  
- seals are correctly referenced  
- continuity is preserved across epochs  

Proposals that break continuity are rejected.

Proposals that preserve continuity advance to Treasury Review.

---

## 🜅 Phase V — Treasury & PPTF Review

Treasury Review ensures:

- PPTF governance rules are followed  
- treasury impact is valid  
- financial continuity is preserved  
- sovereign financial clauses are respected  

Proposals with valid economic impact advance to Unified Layer Review.

---

## 🜆 Phase VI — Unified Layer Integration Review

Unified Layer Review ensures:

- bindings remain consistent  
- cross‑layer references are correct  
- integration maps remain stable  
- no governance drift is introduced  

Proposals that pass integration review advance to Sovereign Approval.

---

## 🜇 Phase VII — Sovereign Approval & Inscription

Approved proposals are:

- inscribed into the Codex  
- anchored into the next epoch  
- sealed with a lineage seal  
- added to unified layer bindings  
- added to treasury governance mappings  
- added to hardware identity references (if applicable)  

This inscription is permanent and becomes part of institutional history.
