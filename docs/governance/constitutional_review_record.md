# Constitutional Review Record

**Classification:** Governance Record
**Status:** Template; no review has been conducted or decision recorded
**Authority:** To be identified from the applicable, approved constitutional review framework
**Lineage:** Review Process Artifact
**Motto:** *VERITAS • SECURITAS • AEQUITAS* — Truth • Security • Equity

**Purpose:** Record an executed constitutional review and preserve the evidence, findings, and decision associated with a candidate constitutional artifact.
