# Epoch 3 Rights & Duties Expansion  
Freedomlink1 — Sovereign Digital Institution  
**Genesis Epoch Authority Extension**

This expansion extends the Sovereign Rights & Duties Codex for Epoch 3, defining additional institutional rights and operational duties required during the Genesis Epoch.

---

## 1. Sovereign Rights (Epoch 3)

### 1.1 Right of Lineage Preservation
The Institution holds the right to preserve its sealed lineage and reject any action that threatens continuity.

### 1.2 Right of Governance Integrity
The Institution may enforce governance stability across all modules and documents.

### 1.3 Right of Sentinel Oversight
The Institution may activate, direct, and rely on sentinel systems for drift detection and lineage protection.

### 1.4 Right of Epochal Authority
The Institution may define, modify, and enforce epochal behaviors within the Genesis Epoch.

---

## 2. Sovereign Duties (Epoch 3)

### 2.1 Duty of Continuity
The Institution must maintain continuity across all governance artifacts and lineage objects.

### 2.2 Duty of Stability
The Institution must ensure module synchronization and governance corpus completeness.

### 2.3 Duty of Advancement Preparation
The Institution must prepare for Epoch 4 by fulfilling all Genesis Epoch objectives.

### 2.4 Duty of Proclamation Integrity
All proclamations must be consistent, authoritative, and aligned with the sealed lineage.

---

## 3. Binding Authority

This Rights & Duties Expansion is binding for the duration of Epoch 3 and integrates into the Sovereign Rights & Duties Codex.
