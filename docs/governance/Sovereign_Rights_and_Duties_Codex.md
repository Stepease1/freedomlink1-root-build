# SOVEREIGN RIGHTS AND DUTIES CODEX

**Freedomlink1 - Sovereign Digital Institution**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Epoch:** 2 (Root Build v1.2)

## 1. Purpose

This Codex defines the inherent rights and binding duties of Freedomlink1 as a sovereign digital institution. It operates as a companion to the Governance Charter and Governance Charter Addendum.

## 2. Sovereign Rights

Freedomlink1 possesses the following rights:

- **Right of Continuity:** to preserve institutional lineage across epochs.
- **Right of Integrity:** to protect hardware trust boundaries and cryptographic records.
- **Right of Governance:** to define, administer, and enforce institutional rules.
- **Right of Amendment:** to evolve its governance through documented, lineage-anchored amendments.
- **Right of Protection:** to defend against drift, corruption, tampering, and unauthorized access.
- **Right of Autonomy:** to operate independently of external systems while preserving verifiable records.

These rights are inherent and perpetual, subject to the institution's governing charter and recorded authority boundaries.

## 3. Sovereign Duties

Freedomlink1 is bound by the following duties:

- **Duty of Preservation:** maintain lineage artifacts, governance records, and epoch history.
- **Duty of Transparency:** record governance changes and material decisions in the sovereign ledger.
- **Duty of Security:** enforce hardware trust boundaries and protect institutional credentials.
- **Duty of Continuity:** conduct epoch transitions according to approved procedures and verified seals.
- **Duty of Stewardship:** uphold institutional purpose, integrity, and responsible delegated authority.
- **Duty of Accountability:** maintain an auditable record of governance actions and their authorizations.

These duties apply across all governance tiers, modules, sentinels, contributors, and delegated authorities.

## 4. Exercise and Protection

Rights may be exercised only through authorized governance processes. Duties must be supported by timestamped records, sovereign signatures, document hashes, module identifiers, and epoch identifiers where applicable.

Attempts to bypass trust boundaries, alter anchored records, or exercise unauthorized authority constitute governance violations and may trigger alert, lockdown, lineage freeze, and Founder notification procedures.

## 5. Continuity and Amendment

This Codex remains in force across epoch transitions. Any amendment must identify the affected rights or duties, specify its effective epoch, include the required signatures and hash manifest, and be anchored into sovereign lineage.
