# POC Epoch Progression

**Last Updated**: 2026-07-13  
**Status**: Active within Genesis Epoch  
**Purpose**: Track the Proof-of-Concept work through the FreedomLink1 epoch lifecycle.

## Overview

The POC Governance Suite is treated as a governed initiative evolving through the same temporal structure that governs the wider FreedomLink1 system.

## Progression Stages

### 1. Initiation
- The POC concept is formalized in public documentation
- Initial mapping connects the work to governance and lineage structures
- Documentation is published for review

### 2. Activation
- The POC is activated through the formal ceremony documented in [docs/poc-activation-ceremony.md](poc-activation-ceremony.md)
- Review criteria are defined through [governance/poc-governance-rubric.md](../governance/poc-governance-rubric.md)
- The suite is linked into the documentation index and governance framework

### 3. Evaluation
- The POC is assessed for governance fit, completeness, and lineage traceability
- Any gaps are addressed through amendments or supplementary documentation
- Evidence remains public and auditable

### 4. Transition
- The POC can be carried forward into later epochs as a mature governance practice or archived as a historical milestone
- Its status remains visible in the governance record

## Genesis Epoch Relevance

Within the current Genesis epoch, the POC suite serves as a foundational demonstration of how experimental work can be brought into formal governance without losing transparency or continuity.
