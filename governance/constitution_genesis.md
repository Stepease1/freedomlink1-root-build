# Freedomlink1 Governance Constitution — Genesis Edition
## Freedomlink1 — Genesis Edition

This constitution defines the foundational governance structure of the Freedomlink1 Root Build.

---

## I. Sovereignty

Freedomlink1 is governed by:
- The Steward  
- The Custodians  
- The Governance Sentinels  
- The Lineage  

Sovereignty is expressed through:
- Signatures  
- Ceremonies  
- Protocols  
- Registries  
- Lineage artifacts  

---

## II. Governance Layers

### 1. Developer Safety
Smoke tests, CI checks.

### 2. Lineage Safety
Governance tests, module/POC verification.

### 3. Sovereign Safety
Integrity Monitor, drift detection, corruption tribunal.

### 4. Continuity
Epoch advancement, key rotation, succession.

---

## III. Ceremonial Framework

Governance actions require:
- Ceremony  
- Registry update  
- Sovereign signature  
- Logbook entry  

Ceremonies include:
- Activation  
- Promotion  
- Retirement  
- Succession  
- Advancement  

---

## IV. Lineage

The lineage is the canonical record of:
- Epochs  
- Modules  
- POCs  
- Sentinels  
- Stewardship  
- Governance events  

It is immutable except through ceremony.

---

## V. Sentinels

Sentinels enforce:
- Integrity  
- Continuity  
- Sovereignty  

Sentinels operate autonomously through CI/CD.

---

## VI. Stewardship

The steward:
- Signs sovereign artifacts  
- Oversees ceremonies  
- Maintains continuity  
- Protects lineage integrity  

Succession is governed by ceremony.

---

## VII. Amendments

This constitution may be amended through:
- Steward proposal  
- Custodian review  
- Sentinel verification  
- Sovereign signature  
- Logbook entry  

---

## VIII. Closing Seal

> “Governance is ceremony. Lineage is memory. Sovereignty is continuity.”
