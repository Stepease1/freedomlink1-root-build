# Custodian Charter

## Freedomlink1 - Genesis Epoch

Custodians uphold the continuity, integrity, and sovereignty of the Root Build.

### I. Duties

- Maintain lineage artifacts
- Execute ceremonies
- Validate governance events
- Monitor sentinel activity
- Protect sovereign signatures

### II. Authority

Custodians may:

- Initiate governance tests
- Convene corruption tribunals
- Propose amendments
- Activate sentinels

### III. Oath

> "I uphold the lineage, protect the sovereignty, and serve the continuity of Freedomlink1."
