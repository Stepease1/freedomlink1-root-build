    PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

    Freedomlink1 — Sovereign Digital Institution
    Version: 1.0 Effective Date: March 8, 2026 Epoch: 2 (Root Build v1.2) Prepared by: Joshua W. Blaz

    1. Purpose

    This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) governs the provision of professional, technical, operational, and advisory services (“Services”) by the Service Provider to Freedomlink1 (“Institution”).

    The Agreement ensures that all Services:

    • respect sovereign lineage
    • preserve hardware trust boundaries
    • align with governance primitives
    • maintain institutional integrity
    • properly assign and protect IP
    • comply with confidentiality and sovereign protocols

    2. Definitions

    2.1 “Institution”

    Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, lineage artifacts, hardware trust layer, documentation, and sovereign state.

    2.2 “Service Provider”

    The individual or entity engaged to perform Services under this Agreement.

    2.3 “Services”

    All work performed for the Institution, including:

    • software engineering
    • hardware design and review
    • Rootstone‑I related work
    • governance module implementation
    • documentation and specifications
    • security review and audit
    • CI/CD pipeline configuration
    • research and analysis
    • operational support

    2.4 “Deliverables”

    All outputs produced in the course of providing Services, including:

    • source code
    • hardware schematics
    • documentation
    • test suites and logs
    • configuration files
    • models, diagrams, and reports
    • governance artifacts
    • lineage‑related materials

    2.5 “Confidential Information”

    All non‑public information belonging to the Institution, as defined in the NDA.

    2.6 “Assigned IP”

    All intellectual property created by the Service Provider for the Institution, as further detailed in Section 7.

    2.7 “Sovereign Layer”

    The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.

    3. Scope of Services

    3.1 Statement of Work (SOW)

    Specific Services, timelines, and Deliverables may be defined in one or more Statements of Work (“SOWs”) referencing this Agreement. Each SOW forms part of this Agreement.

    3.2 Performance Standard

    Service Provider shall perform Services:

    • with professional skill and care
    • in accordance with industry best practices
    • in alignment with the Institution’s sovereign governance and hardware trust requirements

    3.3 Tools and Environment

    Service Provider shall use tools, environments, and processes that:

    • do not compromise hardware trust boundaries
    • do not introduce cryptographic anomalies
    • respect the Institution’s security and lineage policies

    4. Term

    4.1 Term of Agreement

    This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under Section 11.

    4.2 SOW Duration

    Each SOW specifies its own start and end dates. SOWs may be renewed or extended by written agreement.

    5. Compensation

    5.1 Fees

    Service Provider shall be compensated as specified in the applicable SOW, which may include:

    • fixed project fees
    • hourly or daily rates
    • milestone‑based payments
    • retainers

    5.2 Expenses

    Reasonable, pre‑approved expenses may be reimbursed as specified in the SOW.

    5.3 Invoices and Payment

    • Service Provider shall submit invoices as specified in the SOW.
    • Institution shall pay undisputed amounts within the agreed payment period.

    5.4 No Employment Relationship

    Service Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of the Institution.

    6. Confidentiality

    6.1 NDA Incorporation

    Service Provider shall comply with the Institution’s Non‑Disclosure Agreement (NDA), which is incorporated by reference.

    6.2 Obligations

    Service Provider shall:

    • protect Confidential Information
    • use Confidential Information only for performing Services
    • not disclose Confidential Information to unauthorized parties
    • follow sovereign security and access protocols

    6.3 Survival

    Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement and any SOW.

    7. Intellectual Property

    7.1 Assignment of IP

    Service Provider hereby assigns to the Institution all rights, title, and interest in Assigned IP, including:

    • source code and binaries
    • hardware designs and schematics
    • documentation and specifications
    • test suites and logs
    • governance modules and artifacts
    • configurations and scripts
    • derivative works and improvements

    This assignment is perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, and exclusive.

    7.2 Pre‑Existing IP

    Service Provider retains ownership of IP created prior to this Agreement (“Pre‑Existing IP”). To the extent Pre‑Existing IP is used in Deliverables, Service Provider grants the Institution a:

    • perpetual
    • irrevocable
    • royalty‑free
    • worldwide

    license to use, modify, and distribute such Pre‑Existing IP as part of the Institution’s sovereign corpus.

    7.3 Moral Rights

    To the extent permitted by law, Service Provider waives moral rights in Assigned IP and agrees to execute additional documents to perfect the Institution’s ownership.

    7.4 No Restrictions

    Service Provider shall not impose licensing terms that restrict the Institution’s use of Assigned IP or Deliverables.

    8. Sovereign Compliance

    8.1 Governance Alignment

    Service Provider shall perform Services in compliance with:

    • lineage anchoring rules
    • hardware trust boundaries
    • governance primitives
    • sovereign signature protocols
    • security and cryptographic policies

    8.2 Prohibited Actions

    Service Provider shall not:

    • alter lineage artifacts without authorization
    • bypass Rootstone‑I trust boundaries
    • introduce cryptographic anomalies
    • access sovereign keys or restricted materials
    • deploy unapproved tooling that compromises trust or governance

    8.3 Duty to Report

    Service Provider shall promptly report:

    • security incidents or suspected breaches
    • unauthorized access attempts
    • cryptographic or integrity anomalies
    • governance inconsistencies or misconfigurations

    9. Representations & Warranties

    Service Provider represents and warrants that:

    • Services will be performed with professional skill and care.
    • Deliverables and Assigned IP are original or properly licensed.
    • Deliverables do not knowingly infringe third‑party rights.
    • no malicious code, backdoors, or harmful components are intentionally introduced.
    • Service Provider has full authority to enter into this Agreement and assign IP as required.

    10. Indemnification

    Service Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Institution from and against claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses arising out of:

    • IP infringement related to Deliverables or Assigned IP
    • breach of confidentiality obligations
    • breach of sovereign compliance obligations
    • gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud

    11. Termination

    11.1 Termination for Convenience

    Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience upon written notice, subject to any notice period specified in the SOW.

    11.2 Termination for Cause

    The Institution may terminate immediately if Service Provider:

    • materially breaches this Agreement or an SOW
    • violates sovereign trust or governance requirements
    • engages in misconduct or harmful behavior
    • fails to cure a material breach within a reasonable cure period

    11.3 Effect of Termination

    Upon termination:

    • Service Provider shall cease Services.
    • Service Provider shall deliver any completed or in‑progress Deliverables.
    • Service Provider shall return or destroy Confidential Information as directed.
    • IP assignment and confidentiality obligations remain in effect.

    12. Dispute Resolution

    Disputes arising under this Agreement shall be addressed through:

    1. good‑faith discussion
    2. mediation, if mutually agreed
    3. documentation in the sovereign ledger (e.g., dispute entry in the next epoch)

    If unresolved, the Founder may issue a binding institutional proclamation, subject to applicable law.

    13. Governing Law

    This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oklahoma, without regard to conflict‑of‑law principles.

    14. Amendment

    This Agreement may be amended only by:

    • a written instrument signed by both parties, and
    • acknowledgment in the Institution’s sovereign records (e.g., inclusion in the next epoch ledger).

    15. Entire Agreement

    This Agreement, together with any SOWs and the NDA, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Services and supersedes all prior discussions or understandings.
