    BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT (BAA)

    Freedomlink1 — Sovereign Digital Institution
    Version: 1.0 Effective Date: September 8, 2026 Epoch: 2 (Root Build v1.2) Prepared by: Joshua W. Blaz

    1. Purpose

    This Business Associate Agreement (“Agreement” or “BAA”) governs the handling of Protected Health Information (PHI) by the Business Associate on behalf of Freedomlink1 (“Institution”).

    The Agreement ensures that all PHI processing:

    • complies with HIPAA and applicable privacy laws
    • respects sovereign governance and lineage protocols
    • preserves hardware trust boundaries
    • maintains confidentiality, integrity, and availability of regulated data

    2. Definitions

    2.1 “Institution” (Covered Entity)

    Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, sovereign layers, hardware trust boundaries, documentation, and lineage artifacts.

    2.2 “Business Associate”

    The individual or entity performing services involving PHI on behalf of the Institution.

    2.3 “PHI”

    Protected Health Information as defined under HIPAA, including electronic PHI (“ePHI”).

    2.4 “Sovereign Layer”

    The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.

    2.5 “Services”

    Any work performed by the Business Associate that involves access to, storage of, or processing of PHI.

    3. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

    3.1 Permitted Uses

    Business Associate may use PHI solely for:

    • performing Services for the Institution
    • supporting operational workflows
    • analytics or reporting authorized by the Institution
    • technical or administrative functions

    3.2 Prohibited Uses

    Business Associate shall not:

    • use PHI for its own purposes
    • disclose PHI to unauthorized parties
    • sell or transfer PHI
    • modify sovereign lineage records
    • bypass hardware trust boundaries

    4. Safeguards

    4.1 Administrative Safeguards

    Business Associate shall implement:

    • access controls
    • workforce training
    • incident response procedures
    • PHI handling policies

    4.2 Technical Safeguards

    Business Associate shall implement:

    • encryption in transit and at rest
    • secure authentication
    • audit logging
    • intrusion detection
    • hardware‑rooted trust mechanisms where applicable

    4.3 Physical Safeguards

    Business Associate shall ensure:

    • secure facilities
    • controlled access
    • protected storage environments

    5. Reporting Obligations

    5.1 Security Incidents

    Business Associate shall notify the Institution without undue delay of any:

    • unauthorized access
    • loss
    • destruction
    • alteration
    • disclosure of PHI

    5.2 Breach Notification

    Notification shall include:

    • nature of the breach
    • categories and number of individuals affected
    • likely consequences
    • mitigation steps taken

    6. Subcontractors

    Business Associate shall:

    • obtain written approval before using subcontractors
    • ensure subcontractors comply with this BAA
    • maintain a list of authorized subcontractors

    7. Data Subject Rights

    Business Associate shall assist the Institution in responding to:

    • access requests
    • amendment requests
    • accounting of disclosures
    • restrictions or objections

    Business Associate shall not respond directly to individuals unless authorized.

    8. Access to Records

    Institution may audit Business Associate’s compliance with this BAA, including:

    • PHI handling procedures
    • security controls
    • subcontractor compliance
    • lineage‑related operations

    Audits may be conducted by the Institution or an authorized third party.

    9. Sovereign Compliance

    Business Associate acknowledges and agrees that:

    • certain PHI‑related events may be recorded in sovereign lineage
    • hardware trust boundaries may define operational authority
    • sovereign signatures may be required for compliance events
    • institutional proclamations may define future governance changes

    10. Term and Termination

    10.1 Term

    This BAA remains in effect as long as Business Associate processes PHI on behalf of the Institution.

    10.2 Termination for Cause

    Institution may terminate immediately if Business Associate:

    • violates this BAA
    • fails to cure a breach within a reasonable period
    • compromises PHI integrity or confidentiality

    10.3 Effect of Termination

    Upon termination, Business Associate shall:

    • cease processing PHI
    • return or securely delete PHI
    • certify deletion upon request
    • maintain confidentiality obligations indefinitely

    11. Governing Law

    This BAA is governed by the laws of the State of Oklahoma, without regard to conflict‑of‑law principles.

    12. Entire Agreement

    This BAA constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding PHI processing and supersedes all prior discussions.
