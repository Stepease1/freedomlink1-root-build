    Freedomlink1 Advisor Agreement

    ADVISOR AGREEMENT

    Freedomlink1 — Sovereign Digital Institution
    Version: 1.0
    Effective Date: March 8, 2026
    Epoch: 2 (Root Build v1.2)
    Prepared by: Joshua W. Blaz

    1. Purpose

    This Advisor Agreement (“Agreement”) establishes the terms under which the Advisor provides strategic, technical, governance, hardware, operational, or institutional guidance to Freedomlink1 (“Institution”).

    The Agreement ensures that advisory contributions respect:

    • sovereign lineage
    • hardware trust boundaries
    • governance primitives
    • confidentiality
    • IP continuity
    • institutional integrity

    Advisors serve as stewards of insight, not controllers of the Institution.

    2. Definitions

    2.1 “Institution”

    Freedomlink1, including its governance engine, lineage artifacts, hardware trust layer, documentation, and sovereign state.

    2.2 “Advisor”

    Any individual or entity providing advisory services under this Agreement.

    2.3 “Advisory Services”

    Non-operational guidance, including:

    • strategic advice
    • governance recommendations
    • hardware trust review
    • Rootstone-I architectural insight
    • documentation review
    • institutional planning
    • ceremonial or lineage guidance
    • technical evaluation

    2.4 “Confidential Information”

    All non-public information belonging to the Institution, as defined in the NDA.

    2.5 “Assigned IP”

    Any intellectual property created by the Advisor for the Institution.

    2.6 “Sovereign Layer”

    The cryptographic and procedural layer consisting of ledger blocks, hash manifests, Merkle roots, sovereign signatures, proclamations, and epoch transitions.

    3. Advisory Role

    3.1 Nature of Role

    Advisor provides guidance but does not:

    • make binding decisions
    • control governance primitives
    • alter lineage artifacts
    • access sovereign keys
    • override Founder authority

    3.2 Scope

    Advisor may:

    • review governance modules
    • evaluate hardware trust boundaries
    • provide strategic insight
    • advise on institutional structure
    • assist with documentation clarity
    • support ceremonial or lineage processes

    3.3 No Operational Duties

    Advisor is not responsible for:

    • coding
    • hardware design
    • CI execution
    • lineage anchoring
    • sovereign signature creation

    Unless separately contracted under an MSA or PSA.

    4. Compensation

    4.1 Advisory Compensation

    Compensation may include:

    • fixed advisory fees
    • equity instruments (SAFE, SPA, etc.)
    • milestone-based compensation
    • non-monetary institutional recognition

    4.2 No Employment Relationship

    Advisor is an independent contractor, not an employee.

    5. Intellectual Property

    5.1 Assignment of IP

    Advisor assigns all rights, title, and interest in Assigned IP to the Institution.

    5.2 Pre-Existing IP

    Advisor retains ownership of pre-existing materials but grants the Institution a perpetual, irrevocable license to use them as part of Assigned IP.

    5.3 No Restrictions

    Advisor may not impose licensing restrictions on Assigned IP.

    6. Confidentiality

    Advisor shall comply with the Institution’s NDA, including:

    • confidentiality obligations
    • non-use restrictions
    • protection of sovereign materials
    • return or destruction requirements

    Confidentiality obligations survive termination.

    7. Sovereign Compliance

    7.1 Governance Alignment

    Advisor must respect:

    • lineage anchoring rules
    • hardware trust boundaries
    • governance primitives
    • sovereign signature protocols

    7.2 Prohibited Actions

    Advisor may not:

    • alter lineage artifacts
    • bypass Rootstone-I trust boundaries
    • modify governance modules
    • access sovereign keys
    • introduce cryptographic anomalies

    7.3 Duty to Report

    Advisor must immediately report:

    • security breaches
    • unauthorized access
    • cryptographic anomalies
    • governance inconsistencies

    8. Representations & Warranties

    Advisor represents and warrants that:

    • advice is based on professional judgment
    • Assigned IP is original
    • no third-party rights are infringed
    • no malicious code or harmful materials are introduced

    9. Indemnification

    Advisor shall indemnify the Institution against:

    • IP infringement claims
    • breach of confidentiality
    • breach of sovereign compliance
    • unauthorized disclosure

    10. Term & Termination

    10.1 Term

    This Agreement remains in effect until terminated.

    10.2 Termination for Convenience

    Either party may terminate with 30 days’ notice.

    10.3 Termination for Cause

    The Institution may terminate immediately if Advisor:

    • breaches confidentiality
    • violates sovereign trust boundaries
    • engages in misconduct
    • provides materially harmful advice

    10.4 Effect of Termination

    Advisor must:

    • cease advisory activities
    • return Confidential Information
    • deliver any outstanding Assigned IP
    • certify destruction of sensitive materials

    11. Dispute Resolution

    Disputes shall be resolved through:

    1. discussion
    2. mediation
    3. sovereign signature block
    4. inclusion in the next epoch ledger

    If unresolved, the Founder may issue a binding proclamation.

    12. Governing Law

    This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oklahoma, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

    13. Amendment

    This Agreement may be amended only through:

    • written approval
    • sovereign signature
    • publication in the next epoch ledger
